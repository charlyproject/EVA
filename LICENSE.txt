================================================================================
PRIVACY
================================================================================

EVA is designed with privacy as a core principle:
- All speech recognition and TTS run locally on your device.
- No audio, prompts, or personal data are sent to external servers.
- Internet access is only used for optional web search commands (Tavily,
  DuckDuckGo, Google) and to download AI models via Ollama.
